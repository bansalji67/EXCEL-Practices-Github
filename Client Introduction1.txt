Answer:

I start by segmenting supplier spend based on categories, regions, business units, and vendors. Then I analyse trends such as high-spend suppliers, maverick spending, contract leakage, savings opportunities, and vendor concentration risks. I present insights through dashboards and visualizations that help category managers make informed sourcing and negotiation decisions.
